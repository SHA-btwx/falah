الفلاح Falah success

Turn the page

I named this chapter Falah.

It's the Arabic word for success. And the word grows out of farming.

فلح

The same three letters give you fallah, a farmer, and falah, success.

A farmer splits hard ground. Plants. Waters. Waits.

Most days, nothing shows for it. They come back the next day anyway.

So falah isn't a place you arrive at. It's something you keep returning to.

This word is called out five times a day.

  1. حَيَّ عَلَى الصَّلَاة Come to prayer.
  2. حَيَّ عَلَى الْفَلَاح Come to success. Hayya ‘ala al-falah

Two lines of the call to prayer, one straight after the other. Prayer first, then success.

For years I measured success too narrowly.

I studied business, money and the internet. I wanted to build wealth without giving up what I believe. I found some of it.

Then I saw what I'd left out.

  • Faith.
  • Character.
  • Family.
  • The people you affect.
  • The person you become.

I've fallen more than I'd like to admit. What I've learned is that falling isn't the end. You return.

So this chapter has one rule.

Strive for success in everything you take part in.

That's the name of mine.
